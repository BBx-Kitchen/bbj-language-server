<objective>
Give the six composer-launch actions one shared base carrying the kind and an availability predicate,
so the launch call and the presence-scoping are written once.

Purpose: COMP-09 (#616). Four of the six actions are byte-identical apart from one `Kind` constant;
the two SETOPTS actions differ only in their availability check. Per D-06 this ships as a base plus
six thin no-arg subclasses rather than one id-keyed registration: an `AnAction` could read its own id
via `ActionManager.getId(this)`, but that would turn a renamed or mistyped action id into a silent
no-op at click time instead of a compile error. This mirrors plan 93-04's intention consolidation, so
both have one shape and one review story. It lands after plans 93-03 and 93-05 so the launch and
apply paths these actions drive are already hardened — the ordering the roadmap fixes.
Output: `BbjComposeActionBase`, six thin subclasses, one new guard, three re-pointed guards.
</objective>
