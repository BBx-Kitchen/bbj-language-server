<objective>
Give every cross-unit referral a resolution a reader can check.

Purpose: each of the 30 referrals handed a candidate observation from one review unit to another. A
referral whose receiving unit recorded nothing is exactly a finding dropped silently — the failure
DOC-04 exists to prevent. A referral that landed is `already-covered` with a citation. Either way the
reader must be able to check it, which means naming the finding ID, the check line, or the search that
came up empty.

Output: `MAJOR-REFACTORS.md` §"Other Dispositions" §"Cross-unit referrals and their resolution" with
30 non-empty resolutions and a census that sums to 30.
</objective>

<phase_conventions>
This plan is bound by `68-01-PLAN.md` §`<phase_conventions>`. Read that block before starting.

**The resolution procedure, applied identically to all 30.** For each referral entry: read its text to
identify the receiving unit or phase and the subject handed over. Then search that receiving unit's
records in the corpus for a finding on that subject — `grep -n` the receiving unit's own
`unit: *RU-NN-NN` records for the subject's distinctive identifiers (file path, symbol name, config
key), and read any candidate record with the awk extractor before claiming a match. Write one of
exactly three outcomes:

- **landed** — the receiving unit recorded a finding; name its ID and one clause of its subject so a
  reader can confirm the match without re-deriving it. This is `already-covered` with a citation.
- **absorbed as an observation** — the receiving unit answered the subject inside a dimension check
  line or a named sub-section rather than as a numbered finding; name that section or check line.
- **open gap** — the receiving unit recorded nothing on the subject; say so in those words. Do not
  record a new finding; report the gap in this plan's SUMMARY for the phase close-out.

Where a search cannot settle which of the three applies, record the outcome as untraceable, name what
was searched, and count it in the census — never round it to `landed`.
</phase_conventions>
