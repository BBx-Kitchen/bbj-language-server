<objective>
Give every finding that is neither an easy fix nor a major refactor a written home with its
disposition and reason.

corpus — the corpus has three `disposition:` values, and two of DOC-04's categories live in prose
sub-blocks and in the `dedup:` field instead. This plan states that mapping honestly, transcribes all
71 items it accounts for (3 + 24 + 0 + 14 + 30), and keeps them in one section so the two documents
cannot drift into disagreeing copies.

wontfix entries, the 24 not-reproducible entries, the stated zero for `duplicate`, the 14
already-covered entries, and the 30 cross-unit referrals with resolution slots; plus the one-line
pointer in `EASY-FIXES.md`.
</objective>
