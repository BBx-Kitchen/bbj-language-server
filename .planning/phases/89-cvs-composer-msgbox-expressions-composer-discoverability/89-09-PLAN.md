<objective>
Extend the shared server-side cue from addWindow to every `.bbj` composer (DISC-01, #650; D-01, D-02):
MSGBOX, addChildWindow, CVS() and in-code SETOPTS, in plain-text composer-specific titles (D-04, D-05) that
are always visible (D-06). Also teach VS Code's `bbj.openComposerAt` to open each of those composers.

existing decode function, so a cue appears exactly where that composer can act.

are gated on the recorded routing decision and land in plan 89-11. IntelliJ click mapping for `cvs` lands
in plan 89-12.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| built AST → cue eligibility | The SETOPTS and CVS editable verdicts decide where cues appear |
| cue click → composer panel or in-code command | Existing write-capable flows reached from a new entry point |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-89-28 | Tampering | a cue offered on a shape its composer would mis-edit | medium | mitigate | CVS and SETOPTS cues require the composer's own editable verdict; MSGBOX expression calls open compose-and-replace with the original visible |
| T-89-29 | Denial of Service | per-request AST work growing with the document | medium | mitigate | decodeInCode runs only at SETOPTS keyword offsets and reads the existing AST; the structural test over a mixed 5000-line document pins zero parse/update/build calls |
| T-89-30 | Tampering | a stale cue editing changed text | high | mitigate | Every dispatch branch re-decodes the current line at click time and shows `LENS_TARGET_GONE_TEXT` when absent; no branch edits |
| T-89-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | No dependency is added |
</threat_model>
