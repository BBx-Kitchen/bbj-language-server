**The enumerated-line grammar**, frozen by `65-01` and used unchanged here:

```
- [SEC-05][candidate] <path>:<line> — <verdict> — <written line>
- [SEC-05][extra] <path>:<line> — <verdict> — <written line>
- [SEC-05][candidate] <path> — pending
```

`<verdict>` is exactly one of `pass`, `fail`, `undetermined`, `n/a`. `[candidate]` lines are the raw
enumeration, one per line the derivation commands printed plus one per tool script; `[extra]` lines
are real spawn sites the raw greps did not reach, added so the denominator can drift **upward** and
be seen to have drifted. A `fail` names its discharging ID — a new `P65-D1-nnn` **or** an inherited
`P6{1,2,3,4}-D1-nnn` owner from the ledger, which is D-04 rendered as a grep. An `undetermined` names
its `### Not-reproducible dispositions` entry. An `n/a` carries a written exclusion reason.

cross-IDE comparison and the surface closure, Task 3 the phase close-out. The comparison sits in
Task 2 rather than in Task 1 because it needs both legs in hand, and it is the piece of this surface
no single-module review could have produced.
