vscode-jsonrpc 8.2.1 (import from 'vscode-jsonrpc/node.js'; it re-exports the common API): `ResponseError` (has a numeric `code`) and `ErrorCodes`. The codes vscode-jsonrpc itself uses for transport failures are ErrorCodes.MessageWriteError (-32099), ErrorCodes.MessageReadError (-32098), ErrorCodes.PendingResponseRejected (-32097) and ErrorCodes.ConnectionInactive (-32096). When the connection is disposed, every pending request rejects with a ResponseError carrying PendingResponseRejected (lib/common/connection.js dispose()).
