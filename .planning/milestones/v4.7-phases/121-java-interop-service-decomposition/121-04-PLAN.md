    - The first connect() opens one socket through the createSocket hook, wraps it through the wrapSocket hook, bumps generation by 1, and a second connect() returns the same connection without a new socket; two same-tick connect() calls share one socket.
    - A socket failure rejects connect() with an InteropTransportError and opens the breaker; a connect() before INTEROP_BREAKER_INITIAL_COOLDOWN_MS has passed rejects with the circuit-open message without calling createSocket.
    - After the cooldown, one connect() is let through as the half-open probe; a successful probe closes the breaker and fires a registered recovery listener once (after a microtask flush); a failed probe keeps it open and the next probe is due only after twice the cooldown (capped at INTEROP_BREAKER_MAX_COOLDOWN_MS).
