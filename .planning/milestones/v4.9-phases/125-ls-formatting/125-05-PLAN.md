<objective>
Feed the user's formatter settings to the server through both channels a client has: the live
`workspace/didChangeConfiguration` push or pull, and `initializationOptions` at startup (SET-02;
D-09, D-10). The server keeps only the normalized 15 keys; nothing else reaches bbj-ls.

Purpose: SC5's "only the 15 known keys reach bbj-ls, each with an explicit value and indentWidth 2
by default" holds for real user settings, not just defaults.
Output: the intake in `configuration-change-handler.ts`, `main.ts`, `bbj-ws-manager.ts` and
`extension.ts`, and the tests proving both channels end to end.
</objective>
