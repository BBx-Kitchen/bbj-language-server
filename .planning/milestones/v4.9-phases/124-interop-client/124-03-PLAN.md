<objective>
Build the dedicated format/DENUM connection (the "program lane", D-01) and route
`JavaInteropService.formatProgram`/`denumProgram` through it end to end: lazy open, validated
answers from the guard, classified errors from the shared classifier, typed outcomes, and the lane
lifecycle (no fallback D-04, cool-down D-05, own epoch D-06). This is the phase's end-to-end path
through every client layer; plan 124-04 adds per-method latches, the log cadence and the
cancel-always backstop on top of it.

Purpose: INT-01 (the language server can call both methods) and INT-02 (format/DENUM never delays or
resets live parse diagnostics).
Output: `java-interop-program-lane.ts`, the connection/front wiring, fake-peer support, and
`java-interop-program-lane.test.ts`.
</objective>
