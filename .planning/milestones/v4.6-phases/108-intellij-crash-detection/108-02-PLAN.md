<objective>
Turn the probed hook into LIFE-01's crash detection and keep the existing response policy.
LSP4IJ's unexpected-stop hook becomes the only crash signal. ExpectedStopGuard becomes an
armed-token filter. The first crash is auto-restarted once through RestartGate without clearing the
counter, and a second crash within 30 s gives up with the balloon and the banner flag. The status
feed goes back to display and logging only.

Purpose: Phase 97 showed that the status sequence cannot tell a kill from a normal stop. The hook
fires only when no stop() came first (D-01), and this plan builds on the sequence the probe observed
(D-13).
Output: `reportUnexpectedExit`, `applyCrashPolicy`, `requestGatedRestart`,
`isAutoRestartAbandoned`, `ExpectedStopGuard.classifyExit`, the disarm after the plugin's own stop,
the crash and give-up WARN lines, and rewritten guards and tests.
</objective>
