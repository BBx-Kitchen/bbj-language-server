<objective>
Make the use-before-assignment check and scope computation skip a reference that has no symbol,
instead of throwing (VAL-02, D-05..D-08).

Purpose: the malformed `## = 1` shape builds a `SymbolRef` whose `symbol` is absent. The check throws
at `getSymbolRefName` (caught by Langium, which silently drops the whole check for that scope), and
`ENTER ##` throws during scope computation (not caught, the build fails).
Output: optional reads at the eight check sites and the scope-local input-variable site, plus
regression tests in `variable-scoping.test.ts`.
</objective>
