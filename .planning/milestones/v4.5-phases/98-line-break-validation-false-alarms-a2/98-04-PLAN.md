incomplete mask, plus the one residue group whose symptom is a line-break error: the single-line

breaks". That is a validator defect, so the fix belongs in the validator (D-02), and nowhere else.

trailing-comma `PRINT` symptom, a new `line-break-single-line-if.test.ts` with positive and
still-flagged cases, and two conformance fixtures.

grammar plan running in the same wave.
