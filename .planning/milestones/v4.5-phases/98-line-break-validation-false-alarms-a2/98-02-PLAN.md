<objective>
Close the second-largest block of A2 false alarms: the constructs whose grammar rule exists but
cannot match what BBj actually allows after the keyword, so Chevrotain's statically computed
lookahead silently prefers the fallback `ExpressionStatement` and the rest of the line becomes
extra sibling statements. About 64 files across `RESTORE n` (~45), keyword-named `GOSUB`/`GOTO`
targets (12), `EXIT expr` (4) and `LOAD` (3).

Purpose: no parser error is ever raised for these — the file silently gets a different, wrong AST,
and `line-break-validation.ts` faithfully reports the symptom. The fix belongs at the root, in the
grammar and the lexer (D-01), never in the validator.

Output: a widened `RestoreStatement`, a new `LoadStatement`, a widened `EXIT_NO_NL` lookahead, a
branch-target exclusion on the custom NL-sensitive tokens (D-04), three conformance regression
fixtures, and new positive plus still-flagged cases in `line-break-validation.test.ts`.
</objective>
