<objective>
Fix the cheap shapes that remain on list A after the three named groups, and record every shape that
stays with the reason it stays.

Purpose: the phase's fourth criterion asks for a residue that is a decision rather than an unknown.
The three groups before this one removed the large causes; what is left is a long tail whose members
divide into two kinds — a small, local grammar change with a clear compiler-accepted form, and
anything needing lexer work or a new mechanism. The first kind is fixed here, the second is recorded
by shape with a reason category. The candidates the phase context expects in the cheap class are an
option tail on three verbs, one of which has no grammar rule at all, and an input form written
without a space; the candidates research already established as needing a new mechanism are
bare-number branch targets. Two shapes research reclassified as already parsing get a regression case
only if that is cheap (D-10, D-14).

This plan does not drive list A to zero. The rough target is ten; the gate is twenty-five; the point
is the quality of the residue, not the number.

Output: the cheap fixes and their fixture, this group's cases in `parser-keyword-statements.test.ts`,
this group's run appended to `100-CONFORMANCE.md`, and the shape-level residue table with its counts
and reason categories — with the per-file mapping handed to the orchestrator.
</objective>
