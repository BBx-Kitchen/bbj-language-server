<objective>
Make empty brackets legal wherever an array element can stand, with the meaning of the whole array,
and make the type-side bracket shapes parse: more than one bracket pair on a declaration, and the
whole-array marker written after a parameter's name.

This plan is the phase tracer. It wires one thin path end-to-end through every layer the phase
touches — grammar rule, `langium:generate`, the regenerated parser, a conformance fixture, a live
test assertion, the private conformance measurement — and proves it green before any other construct
is attempted. The four later grammar plans each drop their own rule and fixture into the loop this
plan proves.

Purpose: the array-element postfix demands either the literal `ALL` or at least one index between
its brackets, so an immediately-closing bracket fails at every one of the eleven call sites, all of
which reach that one rule through the shared expression path. It is the phase's largest group — about
29 of the 52 files on list A at the Phase 99 close — and it is one rule (D-02). The type-side shapes
ride in the same group and the same regression file because they are the same subject and because the
mid-stream misparse they cause would otherwise keep their files on list A even after the element fix
lands (D-04).

Output: the widened array-element rule, the repeatable bracket-pair property on the four declaration
sites, the three updated reads in `check-classes.ts`, the regenerated parser, the fixture
`array-bracket-forms.bbj`, this group's cases in `parser-keyword-statements.test.ts`, and
`100-CONFORMANCE.md` opened with this group's measurement.
</objective>
