<objective>
Establish, by compiling every keyword of the grammar with the real BBj compiler and parsing the same
text with the language server, which language words BBj itself accepts as names — and fix every word
the compiler accepts and the parser rejects, one word at a time, in the three claimed positions.

Purpose: criterion 3 of the phase deliberately replaces a guessed word list with an oracle. The
fourteen words the roadmap names already work at the Phase 99 close and are locked in by the
regression file; the rest of the set is whatever the sweep finds. The research sweep already ran once
this cycle and found three distinct mechanisms behind the mismatches — a keyword declared in lower
case that never enters the generic identifier-category loop, three uppercase block terminators
explicitly opted out of that loop by name, and a terminal with a bespoke pattern that never enters
the loop at all — plus one methodology blind spot: the plain read position lets a custom-pattern word
degrade into a spurious extra statement with no parser error at all, so the sweep needs a second,
deeper form (D-06, D-07).

Output: the sweep and its recorded result, the per-word fixes in the name rules and in the token
builder, the regenerated parser, the fixture `language-words-as-names.bbj` carrying the accepted word
list, this group's cases in `parser-keyword-statements.test.ts`, and this group's run plus the
rejected-word record appended to `100-CONFORMANCE.md`.
</objective>
