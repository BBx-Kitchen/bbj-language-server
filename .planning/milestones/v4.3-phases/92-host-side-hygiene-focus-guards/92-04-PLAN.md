<objective>
RESP-05 (#500). Decompile stops spinning the 20-second timeout on coarse-mtime filesystems. Before bbjlst runs, the leftover `.lst` is deleted, which makes any `.lst` that appears provably fresh (D-01). The wait then drops its modification-time gate (D-02). A leftover that cannot be deleted fails closed (D-03). The stale-listing test is reworked around delete-then-wait (D-04).

Purpose: on filesystems with whole-second mtimes, a fresh listing can carry an mtime earlier than the call start, so today's gate rejects real output until timeout.
Output: `deleteLeftoverLst` in the vscode-free `decompile-io.ts`, a one-line call in `Commands.cjs`, and reworked and new tests.
</objective>
